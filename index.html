02 — Why aerial

The plain version

A ladder shows you a roof. A flight shows you the whole property.

Most of what matters about a building — the roof, the lot, the access, how it sits next to everything else — cannot be seen from the ground. That is the entire argument.

  • MarketingPhotograph a listing from the air and you sell the land it sits on, not just the front door.
  • DocumentationRecord a site as it stands today, from positions we can fly again next month.
  • InspectionsGet close to a roofline or a structure without sending anyone up a ladder to do it.
  • ProgressRepeat one flight path at intervals and a build becomes comparable week to week.
  • AssessmentSee access, drainage, boundaries and neighbours whole, before the decision gets made.
